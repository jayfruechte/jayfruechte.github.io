This season’s challenge: Fast Together

Each member identifies one indwelling sin that they struggle with. Over the next week, select one day where every member can try a 24-hour fast. During the fast, pray specifically that God would mortify the sin in one another’s life.

Join the group this week

Fighter Groups at Frontier take one card a week. Take this one the way they do, wherever you are:

  1. Read Luke 18:22 on your own first.
  2. Talk it through with a friend, your family or your group. The question is meant to be said out loud, not written down.
  3. Pray for the people you talked with, by name.
  4. Take on the season’s challenge: Fast Together.
